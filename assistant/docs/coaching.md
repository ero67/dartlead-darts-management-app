# DartLead coaching guide: what to practise

Which DartLead practice mode helps with which weakness. Open **Practice** in
the sidebar, choose the game, set the options on the **New game** screen and
press **Start**. The modes and settings are described in the practice user
guide; this guide only says which one to pick. Judge a weakness by comparing a
player's own numbers (practice against matches, recent against earlier), not
against anyone else's.

## Low checkout percentage

Practise finishing in the **Checkout trainer**. Set **Finishes** to **Doubles
only** to drill the doubles themselves, or to the range where the player
misses most (**Low**, **Mid** or **High**). Use **Attempts** 20 or 50 so the
**Hit rate** means something. After a session, **Hardest finishes** lists the
targets missed most: start the next session on those, with **Doubles only**
when the misses are on doubles such as 32 or 40. Compare **Hit rate** from
session to session.

## Weak start of the leg (first nine against the overall average)

If **First 9** is clearly below the **3-dart avg** in X01 practice, the leg
starts slowly. Play **X01** with the **Opponent** set to **Bot**, choose a bot
level close to the player's own average (**Easier bot** or **Harder bot**),
**Starting score** 501, and **Legs** or **First to** 3 to 5. Focus on the first
three visits of every leg. Watch **First 9** on the **Session complete**
screen.

## Missing doubles

If the misses are on doubles, use **Around the Clock** with **Segments** set
to **Doubles**; turn on **Finish on the bull** to include the bull. Look at
**First-dart hits** and **Hardest numbers** in the summary to see which
doubles need work, then repeat. For finishing under pressure, combine it with
the **Checkout trainer** on **Doubles only**.

## Slow around the board

If **Darts per number** in **Around the Clock** is high (lower is better),
repeat it with **Segments** on **Singles** first, then **Doubles**. Fewer
darts per number and a shorter time are the goals; **Hardest numbers** shows
where the darts go.

## Low scoring

To raise the **3-dart avg**, play **X01** practice with **Starting score** 501,
**Solo** for a clean average or against a **Bot** a level above the player's
average, with **Dart by dart** scoring so every visit counts. Add the **121**
game with **Rounds** 10 or 20: the target rises with every successful finish,
so it trains scoring and finishing together. Check **100+**, **140+** and
**180s** in the X01 summary to see whether the heavy scoring is growing.

## Weak under pressure at the end of the leg

Use **121** with **After a miss** set to **Drop one**: nine darts to finish
the target, every round. Raise **Starting target** (61, 81, 101, 121) as it
gets comfortable. Track **Success rate** and **Highest made**.

## Cricket marks

If **MPR** (marks per round) is low, play **Cricket** practice against a
**Bot**, **First to** 3 legs, starting with a lower bot level and moving up as
MPR rises. Watch **MPR**, **Best visit** and **5+ mark visits** in the summary.

## How to give the advice

Name the mode and the settings, say which number to watch and compare it with
the player's own earlier numbers. Do not promise a result. Suggest one or two
changes at a time, not the whole list.
