# DartLead practice: user guide

How the practice (training) modes of the DartLead app work. Practice is free
and needs no account. Button and setting names are the English labels of the
app.

## Practice overview

To practice, open **Practice** in the sidebar. The **Practice** page lists
five games:

- **X01**: 501, 301 or a custom start, solo or against a second player or a
  bot.
- **Checkout trainer**: random finishes from 2 to 170.
- **121**: finish 121 in nine darts and move up when you do.
- **Around the Clock**: 1 to 20, then the bull.
- **Cricket**: against a friend or a bot.

Tap a game, choose its settings on the **New game** screen and press
**Start**. During a game, **Finish session** ends it early. At the end the
**Session complete** screen shows the results with **Play again**,
**Change settings** and **Back to practice**.

## Resuming or discarding a practice session

A practice session in progress is saved on the device after every dart. When
you come back to the **Practice** page it shows **Session in progress** with
**Resume** to continue and **Discard** to throw it away. A discarded session is
not saved to the history (**Session discarded. It was not saved to your
history.**); **Undo** right after discarding brings it back.

## Practice X01: settings

The **New X01 session** screen has these settings:

- **Starting score**: **501**, **301**, **170**, **121** or **Custom** (any
  value from 2 to 1001).
- **Opponent**: **Solo** (you against the board), **Second player** (someone
  else scoring on the same device; enter the **Second player's name**) or
  **Bot**.
- **Legs**: for solo practice, the session ends after 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 or 10 legs,
  or **Unlimited** (play until you stop). Against an opponent it is
  **First to** 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7 or 10 legs.
- **Scoring**: **Dart by dart** (every dart counted, full checkout statistics)
  or **3-dart total** (type each visit total, faster).
- **Who starts** (against an opponent): **You**, the opponent, or
  **Bull-up** (decided at the bull when the match opens).

X01 practice uses double out. The **Game preview** shows the chosen settings
before **Start**.

## Practice X01 against a bot

To play against a bot, choose **Bot** as the **Opponent** and pick a level with
**Easier bot** and **Harder bot**. There are eight bot levels. The approximate
three-dart average of each level is:

| Level | Average |
| --- | --- |
| Bot level 1 | about 35 |
| Bot level 2 | about 42 |
| Bot level 3 | about 50 |
| Bot level 4 | about 58 |
| Bot level 5 | about 66 |
| Bot level 6 | about 75 |
| Bot level 7 | about 85 |
| Bot level 8 | about 95 |

Level 4 is the default. The bot throws its darts on screen one at a time;
**Tap to skip** shows its visit at once. Bots miss like human players rather
than scoring a fixed number. After the match the summary shows the bot's
average next to yours.

## Practice X01: entering scores and long legs

Practice X01 uses the same keypads as tournament matches:

- **Dart by dart**: number keys 1 to 20, 25 and 0 (miss), with **Double** and
  **Triple** toggles (**Double** + 25 is the bull) and **Undo**.
- **3-dart total**: type the visit total (0 to 180) and press **OK**; when the
  total finishes the leg, a dialog asks how many darts were used and whether it
  was a double-out or a bust.

The screen shows **Remaining** and the score of **This leg**. In a match
against an opponent, a leg that goes past 15 visits each can be decided at the
bull.

## Practice X01: statistics

A finished X01 session shows: **3-dart avg**, **First 9** (average of the first
nine darts of each leg), **Checkout %**, **180s**, **140+**, **100+**,
**Best leg**, **High checkout**, **High visit**, **Darts**, **Legs** and
**Busts**, plus a leg-by-leg list. Against an opponent the summary also says
**Match won** or **Match lost** with the leg score.

## Checkout trainer

The **Checkout trainer** draws random finishes, one three-dart visit each.

Settings on **New checkout session**:

- **Finishes**: **Doubles only** (finishes possible with one dart, 2 to 50),
  **Low** (2–60), **Mid** (61–100), **High** (101–170) or **All** (2–170).
- **Attempts**: 10, 20 or 50 finishes before the summary, or **Unlimited**.

For each target, type your three-dart total. If the total lands exactly on the
target, choose how many darts it took and whether it ended on a double
(**Counts as a finish**) or was a bust (**No double — attempt missed**).
Any other total is a miss.

The summary shows **Hits**, **Hit rate**, **Darts per finish**,
**Best streak** and **Hardest finishes** (the targets you missed most).

## 121 practice game

In **121**, check out the target within three visits (nine darts). Make it and
the target goes up by one; miss and it drops by one, but never below the target
you started on.

Settings on **New 121 session**:

- **Starting target**: 61, 81, 101 or 121. It is also the floor: the target
  never drops below it.
- **Rounds**: 10, 20 or **Unlimited**.
- **After a miss**: **Drop one** or **Stay**.
- **Scoring**: **Dart by dart** or **3-dart total**.

The summary shows **Made**, **Success rate**, **Highest target**,
**Highest made**, **Final target**, **Rounds**, **Best streak** and
**Darts per finish**.

## Around the Clock

In **Around the Clock**, hit 1 to 20 in order, then the bull. Fewest darts and
fastest time win. The timer starts with the first dart.

Settings on **New Around the Clock**:

- **Segments**: **Singles**, **Doubles** or **Trebles**: which segment counts
  as a hit.
- **Finish on the bull**: whether the bull is the last target. Not available
  with Trebles.

For every dart press **Hit** or **Miss**; **Undo** takes back the last dart.
The screen shows the current **Target**, the **Time** and the
**Darts at this number**.

The summary shows the total darts and time, **Darts per number**,
**First-dart hits** and **Hardest numbers**.

## Cricket practice

**Cricket** practice is always a match: against a friend on the same device
(**Second player**) or against a **Bot**. Close 15 to 20 and the bull before
your opponent and score on the numbers they leave open (3 marks close a
number; extra marks score).

Settings on the Cricket **New game** screen:

- **Opponent**: **Second player** (with a name) or **Bot**, using the same
  eight bot levels as X01. For Cricket each level shows its approximate MPR
  (marks per round) instead of an average.
- **First to**: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7 or 10 legs.
- **Who starts**: **You**, the opponent, or **Bull-up**.

Darts are entered on the Cricket pad (15–20 and bull with single, double and
treble keys, **Miss**, **Undo** and **Next player**). The summary shows MPR,
**Best visit**, **5+ mark visits** and the number of 9-mark visits.

## Practice history

**Practice history** (open it with **View all** under **Recent sessions** on the
Practice page) lists every finished session, newest first, grouped by
**Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 30 days** and older dates. Filter by game with the buttons at
the top (**All** or one game). Each entry shows the game settings and its key
numbers.

- **Delete** removes one session.
- **Clear history** deletes all practice sessions.

## Where practice sessions are saved

Without an account, practice sessions are saved only on the device
(**Sessions are saved on this device.**). Clearing the browser data or using
another device loses them.

When you sign in, practice sessions are saved to your account and to the
device, and follow you to every device. Sessions already on the device are
uploaded the first time you open Practice while signed in. The Practice page
shows the sync state (**Synced with your account**, **Syncing…**, or
**Offline · N waiting to upload**); **Sync now** retries a failed sync. Deleting
a session while signed in removes it from the device and from the account.

## Personal bests

**Personal bests** on the Practice page summarise all finished sessions per
game, with the number of sessions in the last 30 days:

- **X01**: **Best average**, **Best first 9**, **Best leg**, **180s** and
  **High checkout**. A best average counts only from sessions with at least 9
  darts.
- **Checkout trainer**: **Best hit rate** (from sessions with enough attempts)
  and **Best streak**.
- **121**: **Highest made** and **Best streak**.
- **Around the Clock**: **Fewest darts** and **Fastest time**.
- **Cricket**: **Best MPR**, **Best visit** and **Most legs won**.

Your personal bests also appear in a **Practice** section on your own player
profile. Other people viewing your profile do not see this section.
